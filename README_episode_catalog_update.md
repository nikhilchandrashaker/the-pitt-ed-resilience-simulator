# Episode catalog update: all 30 episodes

This update includes all 15 episodes from Season 1 and all 15 episodes from Season 2, using episode titles, air dates, and paraphrased public episode-guide summaries.

## Dataset fields
- `episode_id`, season and episode number, title, air date
- paraphrased public synopsis
- incident/operational themes
- emergency-management lens and planning relevance
- source type and verification caveat

## Source and method
Episode metadata and summary paraphrases are based primarily on the TVmaze episode guide:
https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/75632/the-pitt/episodeguide

The catalog is not a transcript and does not claim to capture every event in each episode. The emergency-management lens is an analytical interpretation added for this project. If using this for formal research, review the episodes directly and add timestamps and notes from your own observations.

The episode catalog is distinct from the synthetic encounter dataset. No patient-level wait times, staff counts, clinical outcomes, or resource quantities are claimed to come from the show.
